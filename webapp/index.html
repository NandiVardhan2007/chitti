An AI assistant that lives on your phone, not in the cloud.

Chitti reads the notifications that arrive on your phone, works out what actually needs you, and keeps it on a single Today list. Ask it things by voice or text — it answers with a language model running on the phone itself.

Get the APK Read the docs

What it does

Today

Commitments pulled out of your notifications, urgent ones first, with one-tap reply, calendar and reminder actions — plus a log of what Chitti did for you.

Ask

Type or talk. It answers questions from facts you've saved, sets reminders, reports what's pending, toggles the flashlight, opens apps, and handles general questions with the on-device model.

Voice

Tap the mic and speak. Chitti shows the transcript live and answers aloud.

Library

Everything Chitti found in your notifications, plus the facts you've taught it.

Link checking

Links opened through Chitti are checked on the phone and with Google Safe Browsing, then safe ones open in your browser.

Personal details

ID documents are scanned and parsed on the phone and kept in an encrypted vault behind your fingerprint. Chitti can fill them into forms through Android Autofill.

Encrypted backup

Optional, end-to-end encrypted with a password only you know.

Your backup is end-to-end encrypted

The optional backup is encrypted on your phone before it ever leaves the device — the server only ever stores ciphertext. See exactly what the backend can and can't see →
