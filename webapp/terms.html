Your account and data

If you sign in or make an encrypted backup, the handling of that data is described on the Privacy page. In short: the app is designed to keep data on your phone, and an optional backup is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves the device. If you forget your backup password, the backup cannot be recovered. You can clear your data from Settings, or delete your account (which removes the account record and its backup together).
